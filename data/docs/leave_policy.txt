ACME Corp Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year; any additional unused days expire on March 31.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

3. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.

4. Requesting Leave
Submit leave requests through the HR portal at least 14 days in advance for leave longer than 3 days. Your manager must approve requests within 5 business days.
